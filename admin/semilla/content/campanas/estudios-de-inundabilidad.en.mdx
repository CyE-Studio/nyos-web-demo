A development next to a watercourse needs a flood risk assessment before it can be processed. This campaign has now ended, but you can still get in touch with us.

## What it included

- Hydraulic model of the affected stretch of watercourse.
- Delineation of the flood-prone areas.
- Documentation for submission to the river basin authority.
