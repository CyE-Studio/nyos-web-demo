## What we do

NYOS provides engineering and technical assistance services for railway infrastructure, on new construction as well as on maintenance, renewal and improvement works.

Our work is geared to resolving, in a coordinated way, the technical, construction and documentation constraints that are specific to this type of infrastructure.
