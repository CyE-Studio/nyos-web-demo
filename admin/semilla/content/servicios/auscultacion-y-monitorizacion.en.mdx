## What we do

NYOS runs a specialist line of monitoring and instrumentation aimed at understanding the real behaviour of infrastructure, detecting how it evolves and providing useful information for maintenance and decision-making.

Instrumentation is always planned around a specific technical question and the interpretation of the data obtained.

## From data to diagnosis

Measuring is the starting point; the value lies in interpreting the behaviour of the infrastructure.
