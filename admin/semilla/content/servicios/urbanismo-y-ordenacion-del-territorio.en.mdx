## What we do

NYOS carries out urban development planning and regional planning work aimed at bringing together the needs of municipal development with infrastructure, urban services, mobility, the environment and the characteristics of the territory.

We approach this work from a multidisciplinary perspective, relying on mapping and GIS tools to analyse the land and the sectoral constraints and to define planning alternatives.

## Current reference

NYOS is drafting the Municipal General Plan of Santa Marta de Magasca (Cáceres) and assisting with its administrative processing. The work brings together territorial diagnosis, urban and rural planning, mobility, infrastructure and services, sectoral constraints and the environmental component.
