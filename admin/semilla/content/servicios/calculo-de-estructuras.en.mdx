## What we do

The Structural Analysis area at NYOS focuses on the analysis, design and checking of civil engineering structures, with particular attention to linear infrastructure and existing structures.

We combine conventional analysis tools with advanced 2D and 3D numerical modelling when the complexity of the problem calls for it.
