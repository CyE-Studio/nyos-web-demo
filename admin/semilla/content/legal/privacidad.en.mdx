## What data we process

Through the contact form we process the data you give us:

- Name, organisation, email and phone number.
- Profile, type of project, stage and municipality or province.
- The message you write.

To prevent abusive submissions, we may also use your IP address temporarily.

We do not need special category data. Please do not include any in your message.

## What we use it for

- To respond to your enquiry and study your project.
- To send you an automatic confirmation that we have received it.
- To protect the form against spam and abuse.

We do not use your data to send you advertising, and we do not take automated decisions that affect you.

## Legal basis

- Your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time, without affecting the processing carried out before.
- The taking of pre-contractual steps, when you ask for information about a possible commission (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR).
- Our legitimate interest in protecting the form against abuse (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR).

## How long we keep it

We keep your data for the time needed to deal with your enquiry and, after that, for the applicable legal periods. [[PENDIENTE: plazo concreto de conservación]]

## Your rights

You can request access to, rectification of, erasure of, restriction of, objection to and portability of your data. Write to the privacy contact email given above and say which right you wish to exercise.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the [Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos)](https://www.aepd.es).

## Minors

The form is not intended for children under 14. We do not accept enquiries from them without the consent of their legal representatives.

## Legislation

This processing is governed by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on the protection of personal data and the guarantee of digital rights (LOPDGDD).
