## What cookies are

A cookie is a small file that a website stores on your device. It is used, for example, to remember preferences or measure visits.

## Which cookies this website uses

This website does not use analytics, advertising or third-party cookies. Nor does it set any cookies of its own. That is why it does not ask for your consent with a cookie notice.

## If this changes

If analytics or any other non-essential cookie is added in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first, as required by Article 22.2 of the LSSI-CE.

## How to manage cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies from your browser settings. Check your browser's help pages to find out how.
