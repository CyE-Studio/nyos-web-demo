02

A construction project is decided long before work begins

Every construction project comes with constraints: the ground, water, regulations, budget, deadlines. If they are not studied early, they surface in the middle of construction. And by then, putting things right costs more.

That is why we start with the alternatives. We study several ideas, develop the most suitable one in detail and support construction to meet the deadline and the budget.

Small details matter in large projects. A good idea also improves the place where it is built.

04

Seven areas of work for a single project

The services Nyos provides

  • Railway infrastructure

    Projects, technical assistance and monitoring of railway works: tunnels, trackbed, drainage, structures and reinstatements.

  • Roads and highway infrastructure

    Drafting and review of projects, maintenance, rehabilitation, pavements, drainage, road safety and technical support on site.

  • Water and environment

    Water supply, sewerage, drinking water treatment plants (DWTP), wastewater treatment plants (WWTP), pipelines, storage tanks, drainage and the environmental documentation associated with projects.

  • Urban and regional planning

    Urban development planning and regional planning: urban and rural land, infrastructure and services, mobility, sectoral constraints, environmental integration and support with administrative processing.

  • Technical assistance, site supervision and H&S coordination

    Technical monitoring, control of construction, support for site supervision, measurements, quality, documentation and health and safety coordination.

  • Structural analysis

    Design, checking and modelling of civil engineering structures, existing structures and soil-structure interaction.

  • Monitoring and instrumentation

    Instrumentation, data acquisition and data analysis to track the behaviour of bridges, tunnels, structures, slopes and other infrastructure.

About us

Nyos Consulting brings together professionals in infrastructure engineering: railways, roads, water and environment, urban planning, structures and monitoring. We work on public and private works.

We are a multidisciplinary team. We combine recent technology with practical solutions and an efficient use of resources.

We support you through every stage of the project. Each person is accountable for their part of the delivery, and we work as a team to drive the development of the communities where we operate.

Our guiding principle is quality. It rests on effort, dedication and enthusiasm.
