Review of municipal wastewater treatment plants

We review the condition of your municipality's wastewater treatment plant and tell you what needs improving, what is urgent and how much it may cost.

Request information
Status
Current
Period
From 1 November 2026 to 28 February 2027
Who it is for
Town councils and associations of municipalitiesWater service operators
Areas
Water and environment

Many small wastewater treatment plants have been running for years without an overall review. Before applying for funding or putting works out to tender, it is worth knowing what condition each part of the facility is in.

What it includes

  • Visit to the facility and review of the available documentation.
  • Check of compliance with the discharge permit.
  • Report with the improvements ranked by urgency.
  • Cost estimate for each improvement, to prepare the budget or a funding application.

How to request it

Write to us through the contact form with the municipality and, if you have it, the latest operating report.
