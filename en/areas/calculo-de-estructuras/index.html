Structural analysis

Design, checking and modelling of civil engineering structures, existing structures and soil-structure interaction.

Tell us about your project

What we do

The Structural Analysis area at NYOS focuses on the analysis, design and checking of civil engineering structures, with particular attention to linear infrastructure and existing structures.

We combine conventional analysis tools with advanced 2D and 3D numerical modelling when the complexity of the problem calls for it.

What it includes

  • 01

    Design and checking of concrete and steel structures

  • 02

    Bridges and viaducts, box frames and portal frames, buried structures and retaining structures

  • 03

    Review and independent checking of structural solutions

  • 04

    2D/3D numerical modelling and finite element analysis

  • 05

    Soil-structure interaction and analysis of buried structures

  • 06

    Assessment of existing structures and support for repair, strengthening or modification works, with visual inspection by drone where suitable

  • 07

    Specialist support for projects, technical assistance and construction
