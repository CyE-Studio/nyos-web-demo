R&D&I

Applied research, collaboration with the Universidad de Extremadura (UEx) and technology transfer, tied to real design and construction problems.

NYOS promotes lines of applied research and technology transfer in collaboration with the Universidad de Extremadura (UEx), particularly in structural analysis, numerical modelling, soil-structure interaction, instrumentation and experimental validation.

The aim is to turn the knowledge generated into tools, procedures and solutions that can be applied to real infrastructure.
